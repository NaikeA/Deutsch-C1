GERMAN–ENGLISH DICTIONARY DATA

This application contains a frequency-selected subset of 10,000 headwords
derived from the FreeDict German–English dictionary, edition 1.9-fd1.

Source: https://freedict.org/
Download: https://download.freedict.org/dictionaries/deu-eng/1.9-fd1/
Original dictionary authors and maintainers are identified in the FreeDict
source package.

the GNU General Public License version 3 and the GNU Affero General Public

combined work. The full license texts are available at:

https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.txt
https://www.gnu.org/licenses/agpl-3.0.txt

The application's original built-in noun and verb entries take priority over
the imported data where entries overlap.
